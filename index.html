About Us

Since 2016, Tamasha SD has brought the nation's best collegiate dance teams to San Diego. Tamasha spotlights Bollywood-Fusion — blending classical, hip-hop, bhangra, and contemporary — while Sanedo celebrates the tradition and energy of Raas-Garba.

Bollywood-Fusion

Tamasha

Tamasha is our flagship Bollywood-Fusion competition, bringing collegiate teams from across the country to San Diego for a night of high-energy choreography that blends classical Indian dance, hip-hop, bhangra, and contemporary styles.

Raas-Garba

Sanedo

Sanedo celebrates the tradition and energy of Raas-Garba, uniting teams for a competition rooted in Gujarati folk dance — vibrant footwork, dandiya sticks, and the spirit of Navratri brought to the stage.
