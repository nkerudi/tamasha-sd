About Us

Since 2016, Tamasha SD has brought the nation's best collegiate dance teams to San Diego. Tamasha spotlights Bollywood-Fusion — blending classical, hip-hop, bhangra, and contemporary — while Sanedo celebrates the tradition and energy of Raas-Garba.

  • ◆ Nationally-ranked, judged competitions with bid points
  • ★ 100% student-run and proudly non-profit
  • ♪ Proceeds supporting community and charitable causes
