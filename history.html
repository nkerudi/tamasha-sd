History

The story of Tamasha SD, one year at a time.

How It Started

Tamasha SD was founded to give South Asian collegiate dance a home in San Diego — a stage where Bollywood-Fusion teams from across the nation could compete, connect, and celebrate their heritage. What began as an ambitious student project has grown into one of the region's most anticipated cultural events.

Every year, our all-volunteer board pours months of work into producing a night that honors tradition while pushing the art form forward.
